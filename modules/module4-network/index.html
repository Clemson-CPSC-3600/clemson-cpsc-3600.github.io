Network Layer

Explore network layer concepts including IPv4 and IPv6 addressing, subnetting, and CIDR notation. Understand routing algorithms, NAT, DHCP, and the mechanisms that enable packets to traverse the internet.

Core conceptual content for understanding network layer protocols and addressing.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish between forwarding and routing
  • Master IPv4 and IPv6 addressing with subnetting and CIDR notation
  • Understand NAT, DHCP, and ICMP protocols
  • Compare routing algorithms (Link State, Distance Vector)
  • Analyze queuing disciplines and packet scheduling

Topics Covered

  • Forwarding vs routing
  • IPv4 and IPv6 addressing, subnetting, CIDR
  • NAT, DHCP, ICMP protocols
  • Routing algorithms (Link State, Distance Vector)
  • Queuing disciplines
